Our Story

❀ ❧ ❀
Sophia and Alexander first met on a rainy afternoon in a quiet bookstore. What began as a conversation about favourite novels slowly grew into weekend walks, late-night talks, and a deep, steady love. Five years later, under the same soft rain, Alexander asked Sophia to spend forever with him. This day is the next chapter of their story — and they cannot wait to share it with the people they love most.
